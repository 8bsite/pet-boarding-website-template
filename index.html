Three shapes of room

Cats choose rooms the way they choose boxes. Tell us whether yours watches, climbs or hides, and we give it the room that fits.

The Ledge

For watchers. A two-metre ledge at sitting height, a hammock under the sill and the best view of the bird feeder.

€34a night · fits two siblings

The Tower

For climbers. Five shelves up to the ceiling, a lookout box at the top and a rope to get there.

€39a night · fits two siblings

The Burrow

For shy cats and first nights. Low ceiling, a covered bed, a window at floor level and dimmed light.

€31a night

A day at Sill

The same hours every day. Cats like a timetable more than they let on.

  1. 07:00

    Breakfast

    Weighed portions from your own bag, noted in the evening letter.

  2. 09:30

    Window watch

    Blinds up, ledges warm. The sparrows arrive on schedule.

  3. 12:00

    Play hour

    Feather wands and paper bags, one sitter per room, door closed.

  4. 14:00

    Long nap

    The house goes quiet. Nobody vacuums, nobody knocks.

  5. 17:30

    Brushing

    Ten slow minutes, or one, depending on the cat.

  6. 21:00

    Lights low

    The night sitter takes the sofa downstairs. Every door checked twice.

Your first visit

Most cats settle on the second day. These four steps get them there sooner.

1

Meet first

A free half-hour visit, with or without your cat. See the room, meet the sitter, ask everything.

2

Bring home along

A blanket or an old T-shirt that smells of you. It stays in the room for the whole stay.

3

A soft first night

Lights low, a sitter nearby and no new faces until morning. The first night costs nothing extra.

4

Letters start

The first evening letter arrives the same day, usually with a cat peeking out from somewhere.

A quiet house, by rule

Stress is what makes cats ill on holiday. So the house is run for silence, distance and fresh air, and four rules are never bent.

  • No dogs.

    Not as guests, not as staff, not visiting. The house never smells of dog.

  • One cat.

    One cat per room. Siblings share only if they share at home.

  • Own air.

    Every room has its own vent, so nothing passes from one room to the next.

  • Vet close.

    A cat clinic two streets away, on call day and night for every guest.

Questions owners ask

Something else? Call the house, a sitter always picks up: +31 30 000 0000

Which vaccinations are needed?

Cat flu and feline panleukopenia, given at least two weeks before arrival. Bring the passport on the first day; we copy it and give it back.

Can two cats share a room?

Yes, if they live together at home. The Ledge and the Tower fit two; the second cat pays half.

What about medication?

Pills, drops and insulin are given at no extra cost. Write the schedule on the booking form and bring enough for the whole stay.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights. On weekdays we also take day guests from eight to six, for owners who are moving house or renovating.

When can I drop off and pick up?

Every day from 8:00 to 11:00 and from 17:00 to 19:00. On Sundays only in the evening.
